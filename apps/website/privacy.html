Privacy policy

Last updated October 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NearbyOrders handles information when you use the app and related services.

1. Information you provide

Depending on how you use NearbyOrders, this can include your first name, last name, email address, phone number, password credentials, delivery addresses, saved businesses, reviews, order details, booking details, and information submitted to a business through an order or booking.

2. Information created by your activity

We create and store records needed to operate the platform, such as order and booking status, payment state, notification records, account verification state, and security events associated with your account.

3. Payment information

NearbyOrders may store payment method selections, payment status, transaction references, and payment proof submitted through the app when required by a business workflow. Do not submit passwords, one-time codes, or unrelated sensitive information as payment proof.

4. Delivery and location information

When you place a delivery order, we use the delivery address and related contact information needed to fulfill that order. If you choose to save a map pin, its coordinates are stored with that saved address so the location can be used for delivery. NearbyOrders does not need continuous background location access simply to place an order.

5. Device and technical information

The app may process technical information required to provide secure sessions, notifications, diagnostics, and reliable operation. Push notification device tokens are used to deliver notifications to your registered devices.

6. How we use information

We use information to create and secure accounts, verify email addresses, process orders and bookings, coordinate delivery and pickup workflows, process payment-related states, send transactional notifications and emails, provide customer support, prevent abuse, maintain security, and improve the service.

7. When information is shared

We share information with the business involved in your order or booking when that information is needed to fulfill the transaction. For delivery, relevant information may also be made available to the assigned rider through the business workflow. We may use service providers for infrastructure, email, notifications, hosting, maps, payments, and other operational functions.

8. Account security

Passwords are stored using one-way password hashing rather than plain text. Authentication sessions use secure server-side mechanisms. You should keep your password and verification codes private and contact us if you believe your account has been compromised.

9. Email and notifications

We send transactional emails such as email verification, password reset, password change, and account welcome messages. Push notifications can include order, booking, payment, and other account updates. You can manage notification preferences provided by the app and your device.

10. Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain transaction records, protect users and the platform, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods can differ by data type.

11. Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or otherwise control certain personal information. You can update supported profile and address information from the app. For requests that cannot be completed in the app, contact info@nearbyorders.com.

12. Account deletion

If you want to request account deletion or have a privacy question, contact info@nearbyorders.com. Some information may need to be retained where required by law or reasonably necessary for legitimate security, accounting, fraud prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes.

13. Children

NearbyOrders is not intended for children who are not legally permitted to use marketplace or payment services. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the platform, services, or legal requirements change. The latest version and update date will be shown in the app.

15. Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email info@nearbyorders.com.
