About NearbyOrders

Local commerce, built for real neighborhoods.

Find nearby. Book or order.

NearbyOrders is a mobile-first local marketplace that connects customers with nearby businesses for products, pickup, delivery, and services. The goal is simple: make local commerce easier to discover, order from, book, and manage.

For customers

Discover nearby businesses, explore products and services, save places you like, place pickup or delivery orders, book available services, manage delivery addresses, follow order and booking status, and receive important updates.

For businesses

Businesses can present their products and services, receive orders, manage fulfillment, handle bookings, configure payment workflows, coordinate riders, and keep customers informed.

Built around trust

NearbyOrders uses verified email accounts, clear order and payment states, transactional notifications, and explicit payment-proof workflows so customers and businesses can understand what is happening at each step.

Our approach

We are building NearbyOrders around local businesses and real neighborhood needs, with a focus on straightforward experiences rather than unnecessary complexity.

Support

Have a question, feedback, or need help with an order or booking? Email the NearbyOrders team at info@nearbyorders.com.
