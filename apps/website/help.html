NearbyOrders

Help & support

Answers for orders, bookings, payments and your account.

NearbyOrders connects customers with nearby businesses for products, pickup, delivery and services. Start with the answers below. If you still need help, email info@nearbyorders.com.

Orders

How do I place an order?
Open a business, choose the products you want, review your cart, select pickup or delivery, choose the available payment method, and place the order.
How do I know what is happening with my order?
Open the order from My Orders. The order status and payment state are shown there. Push notifications may also alert you when the business or delivery status changes.
Can I cancel an order?
Cancellation depends on the current order state and the business workflow. If cancellation is available, use the action shown on the order. If you cannot cancel it, contact the business or NearbyOrders for help.
How does pickup work?
Choose Pickup during checkout. The business will provide the order status and pickup readiness through the order flow. Pickup orders do not require a delivery address or rider.
How does delivery work?
Choose Delivery and select a saved or new delivery address. Once the order is accepted and a rider is assigned, the relevant delivery information is shown in the order.

Payments

How do online payments work?
If the order uses an online payment method, the order shows the exact payment method selected for that order and the instructions required to submit payment proof.
What happens after I submit payment proof?
The proof enters a verification state. You do not need to submit it again while it is waiting for verification. Once the business verifies it, the order shows the paid state.
Why was my payment proof rejected?
The business may reject proof when it cannot verify the payment. The order will show the rejection reason and, when resubmission is available, you can submit new proof using the supported payment method.
How does Cash on Delivery work?
For a Cash on Delivery order, you pay the rider or business according to the order workflow. Online payment proof is not required for COD orders.

Bookings & services

How do I book a service?
Open a business offering services, choose the service, select an available time slot, provide the requested details, and submit the booking.
What happens after I request a booking?
The booking remains pending until the business accepts or rejects it. You can open the booking to see the latest state.
Can I change a booking?
Available changes depend on the booking state and business workflow. If an edit option is not available, contact the business or NearbyOrders.

Account & security

How do I verify my email?
After registration, NearbyOrders sends a six-digit verification code. Enter it on the verification screen. The code expires after 10 minutes.
I forgot my password. What should I do?
Use Forgot password on the sign-in screen. Enter your email, use the six-digit reset code sent to you, and choose a new password.
How do I change my password?
Open your Profile and choose Change password while signed in. If you cannot sign in, use Forgot password instead.

Still need help?

Email us at info@nearbyorders.com. Please include your order or booking ID when your question is about a specific transaction.
